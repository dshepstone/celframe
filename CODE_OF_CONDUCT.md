# Code of conduct

Celframe is built with students in mind, and many people here will be new to open source.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Critique work and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and accept feedback gracefully.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal or sexual attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Any conduct that would make a student or newcomer feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately through [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/dshepstone).
Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content or block people who break
this code. It applies in all project spaces and whenever someone represents the project.
